BALGOURDE – the addon that tells you what to farm and where to sell (WoW Forever)

• When you enter a zone, a window lists the monsters, what they drop and the drop rate of every item.
• Mouse over or target a monster: its loot rates, its value per kill, and for beasts the required skinning
  skill and the leather you can get.
• Sort by "profitability" to see which monster of the zone earns the most.
• Scan the Auction House: for every item in your bags, the addon compares the vendor, your faction AH, the
  neutral AH and disenchanting, and tells you where you earn the most (cut and deposit included).
• "AH prices" tab: current price, 7 and 14-day averages, lows/highs, trend and history of every item.

How it learns: the game does not give drop rates, so Balgourde computes them while you play (kills, loot,
skinning, disenchanting). The more guild or group members have it, the faster the rates become reliable:
data is shared automatically. A grey rate (e.g. 2/7) means not enough kills yet.

Install: put the "Balgourde" folder into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns, then start the game.
Use: the ball + gourd button around the minimap, or /balg. Something wrong? Type /balg test and send a screenshot.
